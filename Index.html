Join the Private Group Meet the Admins Where do I even belong now? Life has a funny way of reshuffling the deck. We get married. We have babies. We go through divorces. We lose the ones we love. We move to new cities, change jobs, become empty nesters, or simply wake up one day with brand new interests and wonder — where do I even belong now? The tribes and groups we once naturally fell into? Sometimes they just... fade. And depending on where we are in life, making new friends can feel harder than it ever should. But here's the truth — we have ALL been there. At least once. Maybe more than once. No matter your age, your background, your beliefs, or who you love. That feeling of wanting to belong somewhere? It's one of the most human things there is.
